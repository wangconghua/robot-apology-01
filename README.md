A make.fun entry for **The Robot Apology** — a two-line robot confession.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I am sorry I replied-all to the whole office with your lunch order, so forty-three people now know you wanted extra pickles.
> I have learned to check who is on the list before I press send, and I will ask before sharing anything with more than one person.

Line 1 owns one specific mistake; line 2 draws the lesson straight from it.
